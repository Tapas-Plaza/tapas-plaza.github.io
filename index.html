Our story

About Tapas Plaza

Tapas Plaza sits right on the Nerja boulevard, where the sea breeze meets the smell of grilled prawns and freshly poured sangria. We're a chill, laid-back kitchen with a soft spot for big flavours and easy afternoons.

Our kitchen cooks across borders — Spanish classics, Asian mains, Dutch comforts, and whatever new dish caught their attention this week. The menu rotates every couple of months, so there's always something to come back for.

Whether you're stopping by for a glass of sangria, a plate of tapas, or a long sunset dinner with the whole table, you're welcome here. Pull up a chair on the terrace — life's too short for boring tapas.
